# App name candidates: top 100

Companion to H43 in [POLISH_TODO.md](POLISH_TODO.md#L103). One list, one method, so nobody re-proposes a name that already failed. Compiled Sep 12 2026 from four runs (Sep 9 v2 / v2b / v2c, the outside review agent's top 20, and the Sep 12 100-name run). The decision itself is still open; Love Desire (keep) is the alternative on the table and its trade-offs are in POLISH_TODO H43 and MARKETING.md.

## Method

Every name below cleared, in this order:
1. **Domains by registry RDAP** (Google for `.app`, ISNIC for `.is`, Verisign for `.com`). Every pronounceable short `.com` is squatted (50 of 50 checked on Sep 9), so `.com` is reported but never a filter. `.app` free was the hard filter for tiers 1 and 2.
2. **iTunes Search API**, US and GB storefronts: no app whose name starts with the candidate in a couples / dating / relationship / sex-wellness category.
3. **Agent pass** (web search): brand and trademark collisions, couples-space apps the API misses, hidden meanings in EN / ES / FR / IT / PT / DE / SV / IS, English pronunciation on first sight, X handle.

Not done for any name: a formal Hugverkastofa / EUIPO / USPTO clearance in classes 9, 42, 45. Do that on the finalist before buying anything beyond domains.

Ranking order inside each tier: **English sound, then cleanliness, then meaning.** Tiers: 1 to 12 clean (GREEN verdict, `.app` and `.is` free) · 13 to 73 one caveat (YELLOW, `.app` free) · 74 to 87 clean but `.app` taken · 88 to 100 reserve, domains checked only, not brand-vetted.

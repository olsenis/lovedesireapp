Rushed sex, unmet needs, pressure — bodies hold onto that experience. It can show up now as numbness, holding back, difficulty letting go.

Rebuilding takes time and gentle repeated positive experiences. Taking orgasm off the table entirely for a while often helps more than trying harder.

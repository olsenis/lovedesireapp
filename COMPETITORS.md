Compiled Sep 13 2026. Sources: an r/apps thread ("any good couples apps you'd recommend?", 61 replies, read via RSS), the iTunes Search / Lookup API for every store number below (US storefront, Sep 13), Google Play listings, store reviews and review sites, and the apps' own sites. Quotes from the thread are attributed to "a Reddit user" and kept short. ## 1. What couples actually ask for

The thread is the best voice-of-customer sample we have. Grouped by theme, each with the strongest quote.

**Paying twice.** The most upvoted complaint about the market leader: Paired and others "lock almost everything behind massive paywalls or make both people pay separate subscriptions just to sync up." One couple pays $15 for Paired plus $9.99 for Lovefix, "ofc I am the one paying."

**Routine pressure.** "Paired was nice at the beginning but now it brings me more problems if I don't do the routine stuff." Another: most apps "felt either too cute, too gamified, or like something you have to actively maintain, which is usually the problem in the first place."

**Nothing pulled us back.** "Most of them died off after a week because there wasn't really anything pulling you back into them."

**Tone mismatch inside the couple.** "My GF likes it but it feels kinda childish for me." The same person kept Paired for the "cute part" and added a separate app for conflict.

**Two different asks.** The clearest advice in the thread: decide first whether you want "more playful/game stuff, actual deeper conversations, budgeting, or just a reason to talk daily." Apps that try to be all four (Orbs, Pikant) read as unfocused; apps that pick one (SumOne, Agapé, In The Mood) read as thin after a few months.

**Widgets.** Four apps in the thread live on a home-screen widget (LoveWidgets, Petiverse, sharedmood, drawn). The May top-10 sweep found 6 of 10 leaders have one. We have none.

**The verdict on our namesake.** A ten-years-married user's list: Couple Game "very soft", Paired "closer to therapy than play", koopla "controlled adventurousness", LovBirdz "genuinely good", and **"Desire: fine, but kind of forgettable."**

**The shelf itself.** "There's like 40 apps being listed here, and most of them are one guy's side project from the last 6 months." True: of the 14 profiled below, 9 launched in 2025 or 2026 and 7 have fewer than 20 ratings.

Household admin (Cupla, Miiro, Casito, Entwine) is a separate category. We do not compete there and should not drift there.

## 3. The new wave (14 profiles)

Shape: what it is · traction · pricing · does well · does badly · what we take. "Take" means steal / avoid / already better.

### koopla (the thread's "Koppla")
Explicit one-phone card and dice game from Chouic, the LovBirdz publisher; the thread recommendation was the publisher's own cross-promotion. **Traction:** 4.76 (58), Jul 2024, iOS; Play listing 404 in the US. **Pricing:** $5.99 unlock or $4.99/wk, $8.99/mo, $29.99/yr; paywall lands right after sign-up. **Well:** four intensity levels (Glow / Spark / Flame / Blaze), per-partner gender and genitals config plus pronouns, a mandatory mutual-consent screen before play, custom dice. **Badly:** "the game is for inexperienced teenagers", paywall friction, thin catalogue, 58 ratings in two years. **Take:** steal the joint consent screen before a Spicy session and per-partner body config for explicit content; avoid single-phone-only (no retention loop); already better with two-phone live mode, three tiers, double-blind Fantasy Wishes, custom truth/dare authoring.

### Lovefix
AI conflict-repair coach: each partner talks to "Sage" privately after a fight, then a shared summary of needs and next steps. Gottman-based. **Traction:** 0 ratings, Apr 2026, iOS; Play 100+ installs with a mismatched single-user "breakup coach" listing. **Pricing:** $9.99 / $19.99 per month, annual, one sub covers both. **Well:** private-then-shared structure (the double-blind idea applied to conflict), works solo, honest scope limits ("not a therapist, not a crisis line"). **Badly:** no traction after five months, marketing is self-written SEO comparing itself to Paired and Lasting. **Take:** steal the "private first, shared summary after" shape if a repair ritual ever joins the Sunday Check-in; avoid AI-coach positioning (out of scope by app_vision, and no traction signal); already better with a real ritual loop and content library.

### Couple Game: Relationship Quiz
Asynchronous "how well do you know me": answer, then guess the partner's answers, category packs. **Traction:** 4.49 (5,657) iOS, 3.7 (17,100) Play, 1M+ installs, since 2018. **Pricing:** $4.99 to $10.99/mo, $39.99/yr, packs $1.99; **per partner**: "your partner would have to pay as well for you to even play." **Well:** reopens conversation after 18 years married; saved answers become a gift bank; proves the guess mechanic sells. **Badly:** payment failures and dead support ("seems like a scam"), answers leak before both submit, "too much waiting in between turns", ads. **Take:** steal a searchable "things {partner} said" bank from past Daily answers; avoid per-partner unlocks and any reveal before both answered; already better with strict mutual reveal, one sub, WhileYouWait for the turn-wait dead end.

### Idylle
French "anti-gamification" couples space: micro-rituals, scheduled letters, milestones, shared story journal. "Not a game. No points. No challenges." By Hugmify, a slow-dating app maker. **Traction:** not in any store; TestFlight waitlist. **Pricing:** lifetime premium or free scout status, no prices. **Well:** sharpest anti-streak copy in the batch ("turning your relationship into a game of points is often the best way to create fatigue"), an honest "not for" list, a free "Lovers or Roommates?" quiz as lead magnet. **Badly:** vapour risk, French-first site, lifetime-only pricing. **Take:** steal the "not for everyone" block for our About page and the free web quiz as a funnel; avoid lifetime-only; already better by existing.

### In The Mood: Couples & Desire
Each partner privately sets Intimacy / Affection / Not in the mood; a push fires only on mutual match, nothing else is revealed. Plus "Heart Signals". Tallinn. **Traction:** 5.0 (1), Aug 2025, iOS only, iOS 18.6+, 19 languages. **Pricing:** $3.99/mo, $39.99/yr, one sub covers both; the gentle "Affection" mood is paywalled. **Well:** the no-rejection framing is crisp; moods expire after 6 h (12 h paid), so the app has a reason to reopen. **Badly:** one rating in 13 months; paywalling the softest option undercuts the pitch; iPhone-only for a two-phone app. **Take:** **steal the expiring mutual-only mood**: a mood that auto-clears after N hours and pushes only on mutual match is a cheap add to our existing mood card and answers the "who initiates" pain directly; avoid paywalling the gentle option; already better with the same guarantee in Fantasy Wishes plus everything around it.

### LoveWidgets
Doodle to the partner's home-screen widget, one daily question, coins that grow a shared garden. **Traction:** 0 ratings, Jan 2026, dormant since v1.0.1. **Pricing:** $3.99/mo, $29.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime. **Well:** ambient presence on the home screen is the right instinct for LDR. **Badly:** the most crowded sub-niche (Locket 564k ratings, noteit 98k), no differentiation, streak-fed rewards, abandoned. **Take:** steal the widget idea only (today's Moment or the partner's mood on the home screen); avoid the coin economy; already better on Daily and Moments.

### Pikant: Couple Challenges
AI-generated intimacy challenges fitted to "environments" you build from your own rooms and props (candles, blindfold, oil), 100+ positions, 48-hour self-deleting chat, scheduler, coins, widget, weekday-themed daily question. Brazil, married-couple founders. **Traction:** 4.8 (91) iOS, 1K+ Play, web app, Jul 2025, responsive developer. **Pricing:** $9.99 or $29.99/mo, $34.99 or $99.99/yr, two opaque tiers. **Well:** personalisation from what the couple actually owns; cross-platform; highest rating of the new wave. **Badly:** "doesn't work 99% of the time", "generic questions and challenges in a pretty package", no way to surface overlap without someone stating a preference out loud (Habi), "non-explicit" claim on a clearly explicit app, priciest in the batch. **Take:** steal a "what's in the house" filter (candles / blindfold / oil / none) on Spicy dares and Activity Cards so dares are executable; avoid chat, scheduler, rewards, the price ladder; already better: Fantasy Wishes is exactly the missing overlap mechanic, and our pools are curated, not generated.

### BaeSync
Daily check-in where each answers about the partner's day ("how do you think they felt"), reveal after both, weekly four-dimension relationship score. Slovenia. **Traction:** 0 ratings, 10+ Play installs, Apr 2026, no update since May. **Pricing:** free, premium announced but no prices; "one partner subscribes for both". **Well:** perspective-taking questions are a genuinely different design; honest "not therapy" FAQ. **Badly:** no users, a "relationship health score" and a streak on the home screen, "built with psychologists" with no psychologist named. **Take:** steal one "how do you think {partner} felt today?" Deep prompt graded against the partner's real mood entry (we have both data sources); avoid scores and streaks; already better with the Sunday Check-in's five dimensions plus Gottman questions.

### Cherished: Love Reminders
Single-user "field guide to the person you love": one love-language-tailored prompt a day, private on-device journal, weekly self check-in. Partner never installs anything. **Traction:** 5.0 (13), Jun 2026, iOS. **Pricing:** $4.99/mo, $44.99/yr, per person by definition. **Well:** prompts shaped by the partner's love language; strong privacy story; v4 redesigned around "one gesture a day". **Badly:** no pairing, so the partner is a record, not a participant; added streaks and paid streak recovery in v4. **Take:** steal the "one gesture, not a task list" framing for the Monday love-language nudge; avoid on-device-only and streak upsells; already better because both partners are in it and the actions come from the partner's real quiz result.

### SumOne
One question a day, both must answer before either sees the other's; answers feed a shared virtual pet you raise. Seoul. **Traction:** 4.88 (18,486) iOS, 4.7 (162K) Play, 5M+ installs, weekly releases since 2019. The only new-wave app with real scale. **Pricing:** free with ads, gems, $5.99/mo, $29.99/yr, Gold $14.99; per person. Free answers capped at 200 characters. **Well:** the mutual-reveal daily question alone held five million installs; the pet gives the ritual a home; answers become a dated log you revisit a year later. **Badly:** ads between submissions, character limits on your own words unless you pay, "questions become a chore after months", redesign backlash, login bugs. **Take:** steal the year-later look-back (a "one year ago today" Home card from Daily answers, data we already have); avoid ads, character limits, gems, per-person subs; already better with no ads, one sub per couple, an adult tier they cannot ship at 4+, and Memory Lane generated from real history.

### Orbs
Kitchen-sink: chat, stories, mood, daily reflections, quizzes, calendar, to-dos, love coupons, journal, streaks, widgets. **Traction:** 4.89 (9) iOS, 500+ Play, Jan 2026, no update since March; site claims 100K downloads. **Pricing:** $9.99/mo, $39.99/yr, plus paid streak restores from $0.99 for one day to $6.99 for seven. **Well:** widgets with countdowns; the two reviews are LDR users. **Badly:** no traction, monetises streak anxiety, "productivity" in the tagline, twelve shallow features. **Take:** nothing structural; avoid paid streak restores, chat, launching wide and shallow.

### Agapé
One research-backed daily question, blind reveal, categories by relationship type (partner, friend, parent) and life stage (LDR, Parenting, Engaged, Reconnecting). YC S21, Rochester, co-founder is a couples researcher. **Traction:** 4.80 (26,209) iOS, 4.1 (9.5K) Play, 500K+ installs, claims 2M users. **Pricing:** free core; premium $9.99 or $14.99/mo, annual anywhere from $19.99 to $99.99 (heavy A/B testing), one sub covers both. **Well:** blind reveal credited with honest answers; academic credibility; TikTok virality. **Badly:** price is the dominant complaint ("$180/year?! more than my TV streaming apps"), "free questions are starting to get repetitive", "started using the app less" over time, cluttered UI, nothing to do together. **Take:** steal life-stage tags on our question pool (LDR, parenting, reconnecting) and the "no card needed" trial; avoid eight price points and a $14.99 month; already better because the same mechanic sits inside twelve other rituals.

### Cozy Couples
Virtual home: live mood sharing, notes, photos, daily question, trivia, a shared pet and bonsai, stars, streak, countdowns. One developer. **Traction:** 4.84 (44,563) iOS, 4.7 (8K) Play, 500K+ installs, ~$70K/month estimated. **Pricing:** $4.99/mo, $29.99/yr, one partner pays for both; free tier usable but notes and photos expire. **Well:** mood sharing as the hero feature, praised by neurodivergent and LDR couples; a nurture loop that gives a reason to open daily; cheap. **Badly:** "everything people install it for is behind a 40 dollar sub", free-tier notes and photos disappear, "so extremely limited" game count, more mood options is the top request, account and loading bugs. **Take:** steal the $29.99/yr per-couple anchor as the market price for the sincere tier, and a richer free mood vocabulary; avoid letting free content disappear and shipping a games tab with four games; already better on content depth, the weekly calendar, no streak, 18+ layer.

### Cuplido
Picks one real date a week inside the free time you declared, a few small moments, a free daily sealed question, and a post-date "one line each, sealed, revealed together" album. Explicitly no streaks, no scores, nothing overdue. One developer. **Traction:** 5.0 (1) iOS, 50+ Play, Mar 2026, iPhone only. **Pricing:** $4.99/mo, $29.99/yr, one sub covers both, 7-day trial timed so a real date lands inside it. **Well:** sharpest positioning copy ("a list of 750 is 749 decisions you did not ask for"), a "pick what you miss" onboarding, sealed post-date reflection. **Badly:** no users, one mechanic, paywalled core loop, iPhone only. **Take:** steal the post-date "one line each, sealed" reflection on Tonight's Date completions (fits our reveal mechanic and feeds Memory Lane) and the trial rule that a payoff must land inside the trial; avoid single-mechanic launches.

## 5. Synthesis

### What we already do better than the field

- **One subscription covers both.** Still the top complaint about Paired and Couple Game, but three of the new wave now say it too (In The Mood, Cuplido, Lovefix). It is table stakes, not a headline. Say it on the paywall and in the description; do not build a screenshot around it.
- **No streaks.** Two 2026 launches (Cherished, Orbs) added streaks and sell "streak recovery", and the routine-pressure complaint about Paired is the second most common in the thread. Our stance is a real differentiator and it needs to be **stated**, not just absent: "One ritual a week. No streaks."
- **Breadth with a spine.** The blind mutual reveal is in every app profiled; nobody differentiates on it any more. What nobody else has is that mechanic inside a weekly calendar with games, an intimacy layer and a memory quiz. Repetition ("questions become a chore after months") is the decay complaint against every single-mechanic app; our answer is that a different ritual leads each day.
- **Mutual reveal enforced.** Couple Game leaks answers before both submit and gets hammered for it. Daily, Sunday Check-in and Memory Lane all hold the line.
- **The turn-wait dead end.** "Too much waiting in between turns" is a top Couple Game complaint. WhileYouWait exists for exactly that.
- **An adult tier that 4+ apps cannot ship.** Cozy Couples, SumOne, Agapé and Cherished are all rated 4+ or 12+; the only explicit competitor with traction is koopla at 58 ratings, single-phone.
- **Private overlap without stating a preference.** Habi's critique of Pikant names the gap; Fantasy Wishes is that mechanic.

### What they do better than us

- **Home-screen widgets.** Six of ten leaders and four thread apps have one. We have none. Needs an EAS build, so post-launch, but it should be the first post-launch feature, not the second.
- **Mood as a first-class feature.** Cozy Couples built 45,000 ratings largely on it. Ours is a Home card with a note; In The Mood's expiring mutual-only signal is the smartest version of the idea and cheap to add.
- **Personalisation from real life.** Pikant's props-you-own environments, Agapé's life-stage categories. Our pools are curated but not filtered by circumstance.
- **Year-later payoff.** SumOne's dated answer log is what people cite. Memory Lane is better, but a "one year ago today" card is a cheaper, daily version.
- **Cross-platform and responsiveness.** Pikant is the only new-wave app with traction, and it is the one that shipped Android and web and answers every review. Our Android is sideload only; review replies cost nothing.

### Pricing, stated plainly

| Tier | Market |
|---|---|
| Indie, sincere, one sub for both | $4.99/mo, $29.99/yr (Cozy Couples, Cuplido, koopla, LoveWidgets, In The Mood at $39.99) |
| Leader | Paired ~$15/mo per the thread |
| Punished | Agapé at $14.99/mo and $50 to $180/yr draws the angriest reviews in the whole sample |

Ours was $9.99/mo and $59.99/yr (decided Aug 2026): above the indie cluster, below Paired, and twice what a 45,000-rating app with the same "one pays for both" model charges. **Decided Sep 13 2026: annual lowered to $39.99, monthly stays $9.99.** A new app with zero ratings cannot charge more than the one with 45,000; raise later on ratings, which is easier than cutting after the first "too expensive" reviews. The launch intro offer still covers month one.
